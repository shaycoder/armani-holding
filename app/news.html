Visionary Leadership of Dr. Shihab Sha Driving Growth and Innovation

Founded by Dr. Shihab Sha, Armani Holding is a dynamic and diversified organization with a strong presence across multiple industries. What began with a vision to create meaningful impact in healthcare has now expanded into a global enterprise that operates in Healthcare, Manufacturing, Logistics, Warehousing, International Consultancy, Real Estate, Education, and Space Development.

Under the leadership of Dr. Shihab Sha, Armani Holding has built a reputation for innovation, trust, and excellence. The company continues to establish sustainable businesses that not only drive economic growth but also enhance community wellbeing. Each division of Armani Holding reflects the founder’s belief in creating long-term value, empowering industries, and fostering global partnerships.

With a strong foundation and a forward-looking approach, Armani Holding is shaping the future across sectors—delivering solutions that connect people, industries, and nations.

New Investment in Education and Global Learning Initiatives

As part of its commitment to social impact and sustainable growth, Armani Holding has expanded its portfolio to include Education and Global Learning. With a focus on creating opportunities for future generations, the company is developing projects that range from international schools to higher education collaborations and training institutes.

Guided by Dr. Shihab Sha’s vision, Armani Holding aims to bridge knowledge gaps, empower youth, and foster a culture of innovation. This new venture highlights the group’s dedication to nurturing talent and building stronger communities through education.

New Era in Wellness Leadership with Mrs. Gaana Vijayan

Armani Holding is proud to announce the appointment of Mrs. Gaana Vijayan as the new Head of Wellness. With her extensive expertise in wellness management and holistic healthcare, she brings a wealth of knowledge and a fresh perspective to the group’s wellness division./p>

This appointment reflects Armani Holding’s commitment to building a strong leadership team that drives growth while continuing to focus on improving lives and empowering healthier communities.

From preventive care to natural wellness and pharmaceutical innovation, our investments are aimed at making health and well-being accessible to everyone.
